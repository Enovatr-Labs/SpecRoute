# Code of Conduct

SpecRoute is a vendor-neutral, community-curated project. Participation in this project should be practical, respectful, and focused on making the shared templates and workflows better for everyone who uses them.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a constructive community include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting different viewpoints, experiences, and tool preferences
- Giving and receiving technical feedback with care and specificity
- Keeping discussions focused on the work, its tradeoffs, and its impact
- Accepting responsibility, correcting mistakes, and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or conduct
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation
- Any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all SpecRoute project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, community channels, and any public setting where someone is representing the project.

It also applies when project artifacts are contributed to this repository. Contributions must not include proprietary material, private customer data, secrets, or content that violates the dignity or safety of others.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and may take appropriate action in response to behavior they determine to be harmful, inappropriate, threatening, or disruptive.

Enforcement actions may include:

- A private clarification or warning
- Requesting edits to comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions
- Temporarily limiting participation in project spaces
- Removing comments, contributions, or other content
- Permanent removal from project spaces

Maintainers should apply this policy consistently and proportionately, with attention to the safety and dignity of the people affected.
